From Eben, on the 2025 & 2024 vintages

Eben Sadie describes 2025 as an extraordinary, once-in-a-generation South African vintage — a season of deep winter reserves, even ripening and near-perfect conditions across the Swartland and beyond, giving the District Series wines unusual class and poise. The 2024 vintage behind the Signature range, by contrast, is a classical year: restrained, elegant, and true to the estate's long-standing style.

Sadie Family Wines is regarded as one of the most decorated estates in the world, and this release includes several bottlings singled out among Eben's best ever. As always, demand for this allocation will outstrip supply — we'll do everything we can to look after our customers fairly.
