2025 Vintage Release

David & Nadia is a critically acclaimed family winery in the Swartland, renowned for exceptional old-vine Chenin Blanc and Grenache, with ratings frequently in the 93–98 point range from Tim Atkin MW and Christian Eedes.

Following one of the earliest harvests in the Swartland in a decade, the 2025 vintage is a compelling expression of both vintage and place — warm, dry conditions and a commitment to dry-farmed old vines yielding wines of exceptional purity and balance. An intense mid-December heat wave did lead to significant yield losses on some vineyards, so this year's Single Vineyard campaign is vastly reduced versus last year.
