The Final Vintage

Porseleinberg is home to a groundbreaking Cape Syrah that exemplifies the essence of terroir-driven, authentic wine. The extremely rugged and dry schistous mountain doesn't invite easy viticulture, but of course extreme terroir produces extreme wine — a single-minded dedication to producing one benchmark, minimal-intervention Syrah.

Regular scores of 94 to 99+ points across intervening vintages from international and domestic critics including Greg Sherwood and Tim Atkin. 2024 is a classic Porseleinberg vintage — rich and concentrated, combining depth with freshness, while fine-grained tannins provide structure and persistence.
